# Glimpse — Brand System

> Glimpse does not remake a person. It remakes the experience of being loved by them — with consent, dignity, timing, and care.

---

## 1. Brand Essence

Glimpse is the quiet infrastructure that carries a person's love across the moments they will not be there for.

It is not a memorial. It is not a chatbot. It is not a resurrection.
It is the engineered continuity of presence — released only when it is wanted, only as it was authored, only for as long as it is welcomed.

## 2. Category Definition

**Cross-generational emotional continuity infrastructure.**

A new category that sits between three existing ones and replaces none of them:

- Not estate planning (which handles assets)
- Not grief tech (which handles loss)
- Not AI companionship (which handles loneliness)

Glimpse handles **authored presence over time** — the deliberate, consented delivery of a person's voice, hand, taste, and intent into future moments they chose in advance.

Four locks. No exception.

## 6. Emotional Tone

- Calm, never urgent
- Warm, never sentimental
- Specific, never abstract
- Restrained, never performative
- Confident, never clinical
- Heavenly, never spiritual
- Modern, never trendy
- Human, never cute

The Glimpse voice sounds like a thoughtful older relative who happens to be a great editor.

## 7. Visual Language

**Mood:** the hour before sunrise. Linen, paper, warm light, soft glass.
**Composition:** centered, generous whitespace, one focal object, never busy.
**Texture:** matte paper, brushed brass, soft cotton, frosted glass.
**Photography:** real hands, real rooms, real ages. No stock smiles. No staged grief.
**UI surface:** glassmorphism used quietly — a single translucent layer, not stacked. Soft inner shadow, never neon.
**Forbidden visuals:** halos, doves, clouds, urns, gravestones, fading photos, holograms, ghosts, blue chat bubbles, "AI face" portraits, sparkle effects.

## 9. Typography

- **Display / Editorial:** *GT Sectra* or *Tiempos Headline* — serif with weight, used for the human voice (author quotes, manifesto, hero lines).
- **UI / Body:** *Inter* or *Söhne* — neutral, generous tracking, never bold for emphasis (use weight 500 max in body).
- **Accent / System:** *JetBrains Mono* at small sizes only — used exclusively for system labels (timestamps, status, "scheduled for").

Rules:
- Headlines never end in a period.
- Body copy never uses exclamation marks.
- Never mix two serifs. Never use italic for emphasis — use a new line.

## 10. UI Design Principles

1. **One thing on screen.** A Glimpse moment is never competing with a notification.
2. **Time is a first-class object.** Every screen shows *when*, not just *what*.
3. **The user is always one tap from stopping.** Kill switch lives in the chrome, not the settings.
4. **The author is named, the system is silent.** "From Dad" — never "From Glimpse AI."
5. **Empty states are not empty.** They explain what this place will hold.
6. **No streaks. No gamification. No badges.** Ever.
7. **Confirm soft, undo generous.** Every destructive action is reversible for 30 days.

## 11. Motion Principles

- Default ease: `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.0, 0.2, 1)` at 480ms — slower than a typical app on purpose.
- Glimpses arrive with a *settle*, not a pop. Fade up 12px, opacity 0→1 over 700ms.
- No bounce. No spring. No confetti. No celebration animation in the entire product.
- Page transitions: cross-dissolve, never slide.
- Loading: a slow brass hairline, not a spinner.

## 12. Copywriting Rules

- Write like you're writing to one person, in a room, at dusk.
- Lead with the recipient's life, not the product.
- Use **second person** for the author flow. Use **third person** for the recipient flow ("Your father chose this for today.").
- Sentences under 14 words on hero surfaces.
- No "AI," no "powered by," no "leveraging," no "unlock," no "experience" as a verb.
- Numbers spelled out under ten. Dates written long ("April 14th, 2042"), never numeric.
- Always name the human. Never name the model.

## 13. Forbidden Words and Phrases

| Never say | Why |
|---|---|
| Bring back, resurrect, revive, recreate | Implies the person returns. They do not. |
| Avatar, hologram, digital twin, clone, replica | Wrong category. |
| Chat with the deceased, talk to your loved one | Suggests live conversation with the dead. We don't do this. |
| AI-powered, AI-generated, our AI | The author is the author. The AI is plumbing. |
| Forever, eternal, immortal | We are time-bound. The contract ends. |
| Companion, friend, buddy | We are not companionship. |
| Memorial, tribute, in loving memory | Wrong shelf. |
| Unlock, discover, experience, journey | Startup-speak. |
| Loved one (used as a noun) | Generic. Use the name or the relationship. |
| Heaven, angel, soul, spirit | Religious overreach. |

## 19. Short Manifesto

We don't believe a person can be recreated. We don't try.

We believe a person can be *carried* — through the voice they recorded for a granddaughter not yet born, the letter they wrote for a morning they knew would be hard, the small physical object they chose for a milestone they would not live to see.

That carrying has rules. The author writes it. The recipient welcomes it. A human reads it back. The system can stop. Nothing is forever, because nothing real is.

Glimpse is the studio and the infrastructure for that work.

## 20. Long Manifesto

There is a moment, often quiet, often late, when someone realizes that the most important things they have to give are not assets.

They are the way they said good morning. The pause before the toast. The handwriting on the inside of a book. The small ritual nobody else will remember to do. These things are not in a will. They cannot be transferred by a notary. And when the person is gone, they are gone — not because they had to be, but because there has never been a kind, consented, time-aware way to carry them forward.

Glimpse is built for that gap.

We are not a memorial site. A memorial is a place where you go to remember. Glimpse is something different: a quiet system that, on a day the author chose, releases a moment the author authored, to a recipient who has already said *yes, I want this today.* The voice on a wedding morning. The letter the day after a loss. The pastry recipe that arrives, in handwriting, the week a new mother comes home from the hospital. The single line, in a familiar voice, that says *I would have wanted to be there.*

We are not an AI companion. We are not a chatbot dressed in someone's clothes. We do not invent words the author did not write. We do not host live conversations with the dead. The model is plumbing. The author is the author. Every Glimpse is something a real person sat down and made, intending it for the moment it arrives.

We are not forever. Every contract ends. Every recipient can pause, close, or hand back. Every family can wind it down. The kill switch is not buried in settings. It is in the chrome of every screen, because dignity is not a feature, it is a default.

What we are is infrastructure. The boring, careful, human-reviewed plumbing of authored presence over time. Voice samples held with the care of a vault. Writing samples versioned like code. Recipient signals — *not now, not this year, I'm doing okay, I miss them today* — that move at the speed of consent, not the speed of an algorithm. A human, every time, before anything is sent.

The work is small, and the work is large. Small, because each Glimpse is one moment for one person. Large, because across a lifetime, across a family, across generations, those moments are the actual texture of being loved.

We think someone should build this carefully. We are building it carefully.

Glimpse does not remake a person.
It remakes the experience of being loved by them — with consent, dignity, timing, and care.

## Implementation Notes

**Replit / web build**
- Tailwind tokens for the palette above (paste table into `tailwind.config.js` under `extend.colors`).
- Body line-height 1.6 minimum. Headline line-height 1.15.
- Default page background: `--g-paper`. Cards on `--g-vellum`. Borders 1px `--g-mist`.
- No shadow above `0 8px 24px -12px rgba(26,26,31,0.12)`. Anything heavier reads as "app," not as Glimpse.

**Figma**
- Create a `Glimpse / Foundations` library: Color, Type, Spacing (4pt grid), Elevation (three levels: paper, vellum, glass).
- Build one master component per archetype: `Glimpse Card`, `Scheduled Marker`, `Author Quote`, `Recipient Check-in`, `Kill Switch Bar`.
- Glass component: one layer, 18% white, 24px blur, 1px inside stroke at 8% ink. Never stack.

**Canva (for decks and one-pagers)**
- Lock the palette as a brand kit. Lock GT Sectra + Inter as the only two fonts.
- Use the *editorial magazine* layout family, not the *pitch deck* family. Generous margins. One image per spread.

**Slides (investor)**
- Title slide: black ink on paper, single sentence, no logo lockup top-right. Logo only on the back cover.
- Avoid stock photography entirely. Commission or use the prompt library (Stage 8).
